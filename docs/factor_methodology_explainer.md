# Factor Methodology Explainer

Plain-language copy for `/factors` (one paragraph per factor).

Each section:
## FACTOR_ID - Display label
- **Summary:** ...

## MARKET - Market Beta Exposure
- **Summary:** This ranking shows how much a theme tends to move with the overall stock market. We compare each theme’s recent price moves to the broad market (using the SPY ETF as a stand-in). A higher score means the theme usually rises and falls in step with the market; a lower score means it often does its own thing.

## MOMENTUM - Momentum Exposure
- **Summary:** This measures whether a theme acts like recent market winners. We track how it behaves relative to a momentum-style ETF (MTUM) compared with the broad market (SPY). Higher scores mean the theme has been riding the same trends as stocks that have been leading lately.

## SMALL_CAP - Small-Cap Exposure
- **Summary:** This shows how much a theme leans toward smaller companies versus big, established names. We use small-cap (IWM) and broad market (SPY) ETFs as guides. A higher score means the theme’s stocks tend to behave more like the small-cap side of the market.

## GROWTH_DURATION - Growth / Duration Exposure
- **Summary:** This captures growth-style behavior—companies whose value depends more on future earnings than on today’s profits. We compare growth (IWF) and value (IWD) ETFs. Higher scores mean the theme lines up more with growth leaders than with traditional value stocks.

## VALUE_CYCLICAL - Value / Cyclical Exposure
- **Summary:** This looks at value and cyclical tilts—the kinds of stocks that often do well when the economy is expanding or when investors favor cheaper, more “old economy” names. We use value (IWD) versus growth (IWF) ETFs. Higher scores mean more of that value/cyclical flavor in how the theme moves.

## QUALITY_DEFENSIVE - Quality / Defensive Exposure
- **Summary:** This reflects steadier, higher-quality companies—typically profitable businesses that don’t swing as wildly as speculative names. We compare a quality ETF (QUAL) to the broad market (SPY). Higher scores mean the theme behaves more like that calmer, quality end of the market.

## SPECULATIVE_BETA - Speculative Beta Exposure
- **Summary:** This gauges how “risk-on” and volatile a theme feels compared with safer, low-volatility stocks. We contrast high-beta (SPHB) and low-volatility (SPLV) ETFs. A higher score means the theme often moves like the more aggressive, swing-prone part of the market.

## FALLING_RATE - Falling-Rate Sensitivity
- **Summary:** This shows whether a theme tends to do better when interest rates are falling and long-term bonds are rallying. We use the long-term Treasury ETF (TLT) as a simple signal for that environment. Higher scores mean the theme has historically leaned in that direction.

## CREDIT_RISK_ON - Credit Risk-On Sensitivity
- **Summary:** This measures sensitivity to “credit risk-on” conditions—when investors are comfortable taking more credit risk and high-yield bonds outperform safer corporate bonds. We compare high-yield (HYG) and investment-grade (LQD) ETFs. Higher scores mean the theme has tended to move with that risk-on credit mood.

## INFLATION_COMMODITY - Inflation / Commodity Sensitivity
- **Summary:** This captures how a theme reacts when commodities and inflation pressures are in focus. We compare a broad commodities ETF (DBC) to the overall market (SPY). Higher scores mean the theme has often moved in line with commodity- and inflation-friendly periods.

## STRONG_DOLLAR - Strong-Dollar Sensitivity
- **Summary:** This shows whether a theme tends to benefit when the U.S. dollar is strengthening. We use a dollar ETF (UUP) as the reference. Higher scores mean the theme’s returns have historically lined up more with a stronger dollar.

## AI_INNOVATION - AI / Innovation Narrative Exposure
- **Summary:** This highlights themes that move with the AI and innovation story—not just general market ups and downs. We compare an AI-focused ETF (AIQ) to the broad market (SPY). Higher scores mean the theme has behaved more like stocks tied to that narrative.

## CRYPTO - Crypto Sensitivity
- **Summary:** This shows how much a theme’s price action lines up with crypto appetite. We use a Bitcoin ETF (IBIT) versus the broad market (SPY) as a simple proxy. Higher scores mean the theme has often risen and fallen alongside crypto-linked risk sentiment.

## MEME_RETAIL - Meme / Retail Speculation Exposure
- **Summary:** This reflects retail-driven, high-hype trading—the kind of sharp moves often seen in popular speculative ETFs. We use ARKK versus the broad market (SPY) as a guide. Higher scores mean the theme has tended to behave more like that speculative, attention-driven corner of the market.

## UNPROFITABLE_GROWTH - Unprofitable Growth Exposure
- **Summary:** This measures tilt toward fast-growing companies that may not yet be profitable—story stocks rather than steady earners. We compare speculative growth (ARKK) to broad growth (IWF). Higher scores mean the theme moves more in line with that unprofitable-growth style.
